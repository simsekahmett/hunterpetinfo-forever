Its code is written from scratch. It follows the idea of
ClassicHunterPetInfo by Spielstein, a tooltip and a search window for
tameable beasts, and shares no code with it. Its data comes from the projects
below.

## QuestieDB / Questie

`Data/Beasts.lua` and `Data/BeastNames.lua` are generated from QuestieDB for
WoW Forever: each beast's name, level, zone and position, and its name in the
other languages.

Questie's license allows others to copy from it under the terms of the GPLv3.
Questie's license also notes that not every historic contributor has agreed
to a license, see its `LICENSE.md`.

## ClassicHunterPetInfo

Which NPCs are tameable, their family, their attack speed and the abilities
they teach, in `Data/Beasts.lua`, are taken from the Classic tables of
ClassicHunterPetInfo by Spielstein, which were themselves compiled from
Petopia (wow-petopia.com). The abilities a family lost in Forever are left out.

## Family data

`Data/Families.lua` is written by hand from what Blizzard and community
guides published about the hunter pet families of WoW Forever.
